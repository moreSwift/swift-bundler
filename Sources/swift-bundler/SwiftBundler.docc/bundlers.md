# Bundlers

Swift Bundler supports a variety of bundle formats.

## Overview

Swift Bundler offers a range of different bundlers tailored for different
platforms and distribution scenarios.

All of our bundlers are usable without any platform-specific configuration, but
many of them have dedicated configuration options for tuning bundler-specific
behaviour.
